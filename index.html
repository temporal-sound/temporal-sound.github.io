“On the dancefloor we converge into one unified blorb — a zoological concoction of horses, camels, & geese.”

AlooOoo! We are Temporal — a Portland-based collective dedicated to throwing vibrant parties with kindred spirits. A group of travelers with roots all over the world, bound together by a shared love of music. Our sound traverses many realms: from deep, driving & powerful to mellow, soulful, & smooth.

With an ear for the abstract and an eye for the odd, we infuse our events with love, curiosity, and a sprinkle of silliness. At the core of it all is self-expression: music as a means to share, to connect, and to be free. Join us and you'll get it; you won't regret it ✨
